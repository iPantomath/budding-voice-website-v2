# Writing Rules

Every page should:

- Sound encouraging and professional.
- Focus on transformation rather than features.
- Use clear, concise language.
- End with a single primary call-to-action.
- Avoid exaggerated marketing claims.
- Maintain a warm and trustworthy tone.

The goal is to help visitors feel informed, confident and ready to take the next step.
