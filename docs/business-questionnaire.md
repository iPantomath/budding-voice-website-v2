## Section B — About Your Business

### 1. Tell us about your business.

Budding Voice is a Communication & Leadership Centre that helps children, teenagers and working adults find their voice, build confidence, develop character and connect meaningfully with others. Our core youth programmes use public speaking as the entry point, while also developing communication, critical thinking, storytelling, teamwork, leadership and self-expression. We work directly with families, schools, preschools, enrichment centres and organisations through regular classes, collaborative programmes, workshops and training.

**Brand essence:** Confidence, Character, Connection.

**Vision:** To raise confident, expressive young leaders who know their potential.

**Learning approach:** Simple. Human. Memorable. Students learn through questions, activities, reflection, practice and real speaking opportunities rather than lectures alone. (Asher: Experiential, Peer Learning)

### 2. What products or services do you provide?

Our main services include:

- **Kaizen programme** - for young children, focusing on brave expression, basic communication, imagination and character.
  - *Note for Website page creation: The Kaizen page should be Children appealing for 4-7 years old. The visitors will be mainly parents but they might show it to the children to attract their attention to attend the class.*

- **Orator programme** - for primary-age learners, focusing on speech structure, storytelling, vocal delivery, body language, confidence and thinking skills.
  - *Note for Website page creation: The Orator page should be Children appealing for 8-12 years old, primary school students. The visitors will be mainly parents but they might show it to the children to attract their attention to attend the class.*

- **Apex programme** - for teenagers, focusing on purposeful communication, critical thinking, leadership, presentation skills and personal development.
  - *Note for Website page creation: The Apex page should be Children appealing for 13-17 years old, secondary school students. The visitors will be mainly parents but they might show it to the children to attract their attention to attend the class.*

- **Kaizen/Orator/Apex** - Four-month, 16-week youth learning journeys, normally ending with a showcase or open sharing so students can apply what they have learned.

- **Collaboration/Partnership** - Preschool and school partnerships, including curriculum planning, coach preparation, learning materials, student progress direction and showcase preparation.

- **Bootcamps** - public speaking workshops and youth leadership experiences. One-to-one communication and presentation training. (Ad-Hoc, Holiday Programme, Miscellaneous)

- **Corporate training** - in communication, presentation, team collaboration, mentoring and facilitation.

### 3. Which products/services are your biggest revenue generators?

The primary revenue drivers are regular youth programmes at the Budding Voice centre and recurring programmes delivered through school or preschool partners. Four-month, 16-class programme packages provide the most stable recurring revenue. Workshops, bootcamps, one-to-one training and corporate programmes are important secondary revenue streams.

> ⚠️ The exact revenue ranking should be confirmed using the latest management figures before the website presents any "most popular" programme claims.

### 4. What makes your business different from competitors?

Budding Voice does more than teach children to memorise a speech and perform it once. We develop the child behind the speech. Our programmes combine communication skills with character, confidence, critical thinking and connection.

- Activity-based and gamified learning that keeps young learners involved.
- Age-progressive programmes from early childhood to the teenage years.
- Facilitation over lecturing: students think, discuss, discover, practise, receive validation and apply the learning.
- Individual feedback and visible growth, not exam-based grading alone.
- Real speaking platforms through class presentations, showcases and community experiences.
- A supportive environment where simple English is accepted and developed according to each learner's level.
- Strong collaboration with parents, schools and partner centres rather than treating each class as an isolated lesson.

### 5. Why should customers choose you?

Parents and partners should choose Budding Voice because we help learners speak with purpose, not merely speak loudly. We create a safe and positive space where students can gradually become clearer, braver and more thoughtful communicators. Our coaches provide structure, encouragement and practical opportunities, while our programmes are designed to show real progress in confidence, expression, communication and character.

## Section D — Your Customers

### Who are your ideal customers?

Budding Voice serves both individual families and organisational partners.

- **Primary B2C audience:** Parents who want their children or teenagers to become more confident, expressive, independent and effective communicators.
- **Primary B2B audience:** Preschool owners, school leaders, enrichment-centre operators and educators looking for a structured communication or public speaking programme.
- **Secondary B2B audience:** Business owners, directors, HR teams and organisations seeking communication, presentation, facilitation or team-development training.
- **Tertiary B2C audience:** Individual coaching clients looking for NLP/Business/Personal coaching services.

### Age:
Main youth learners: approximately 4–17 years old. Secondary audiences include university-age learners, working adults, business owners and corporate teams.

### Location:
Klang Valley, with a strong base in Bandar Mahkota Cheras and surrounding areas such as Cheras, Kajang, Balakong and Puchong. Through collaborations, the brand can also serve families in Mont Kiara, Publika, Desa ParkCity and other parts of Greater Kuala Lumpur. Online programmes can reach learners across Malaysia.

### Occupation:
For B2C: working parents, professionals, entrepreneurs and families with school-going children. For B2B: preschool or school owners, principals, teachers, enrichment-centre operators, HR managers, learning and development teams, business owners and directors.

### Business or Consumer?
Both. Budding Voice operates as a B2C education provider for families and a B2B programme/training partner for schools, preschools, enrichment centres and companies.

### Common problems they face:
The website should speak directly to these common concerns:

- The child is shy, nervous or afraid to speak in front of others.
- The child has ideas but struggles to express them clearly or organise a message.
- The learner relies too much on memorisation and does not sound natural or confident.
- Parents want improvement in English communication, vocabulary, voice, body language and social confidence.
- Teenagers need stronger critical thinking, leadership, presentation and interpersonal skills.
- Parents are unsure how to measure progress beyond school grades.
- Schools want an engaging, structured programme but may lack specialised coaches, curriculum and showcase support.
- Adults and teams may have technical knowledge but struggle to present, facilitate, influence or connect with an audience.

## Section K — Additional Information

**Anything else you'd like us to know?**

The website must present Budding Voice as more than a conventional public speaking class. The larger identity is a Communication & Leadership Centre that uses speaking as a practical way to build confidence, character, critical thinking and meaningful connection.

- The website has three key audiences: parents, education partners and corporate decision-makers. Each audience needs a clear pathway and call to action.
- The primary brand message should remain child- and learner-centred: help every learner find their voice, know their potential and communicate with purpose.
- Use the brand essence "Confidence, Character, Connection" consistently.
- Show programmes by age so parents can quickly understand the learner journey from Kaizen to Orator to Apex and beyond.Apex
- Use real student stories, parent testimonials, school-partner feedback, class photos and short videos to build trust.
- The tone should be warm, human, encouraging and professional. Avoid language that feels too academic, too childish, overly sales-driven or full of jargon.
- The website should be video-friendly because demonstrations of student growth and class energy communicate the experience more effectively than text alone.
- Every page should guide visitors towards one practical action: enquire, book a trial, WhatsApp the team, explore a programme or discuss a partnership.
- Consent and privacy must be reviewed carefully before using any child's image, video, audio recording, testimonial or learning data on the website.
- The website should be designed to scale as Budding Voice grows through new locations, partner schools, licensing-style collaborations and additional programmes.
