## PART 1 — Understand the Business

Let the founders tell the story naturally. Do not interrupt too quickly; use the draft below only as a guide.

### Tell me your business story from the beginning.

**Proposed Budding Voice response:**

Budding Voice began with a simple belief: public speaking should do more than prepare a child to perform on stage. It should help a child become confident, understand their own thoughts, express ideas clearly and connect meaningfully with others.

We initially focused on helping children overcome shyness and build confidence through speaking activities. As we worked with more learners, parents, coaches, preschools and schools, we realised that speaking was only the starting point. A child may learn how to deliver a speech, but lasting growth happens when the child also discovers what matters to them, recognises what may be holding them back, learns to make decisions and takes ownership of improvement.

This shaped Budding Voice into a Communication & Leadership Centre. Today, our programmes combine public speaking with self-discovery, character development, critical thinking, teamwork, accountability and real opportunities to practise. Through regular classes, school partnerships, workshops and showcases, we aim to build a supportive community where learners find their voice and grow into purposeful communicators.

### Why did you start?

**Proposed Budding Voice response:**

We saw many children who had ideas and potential but lacked the confidence, language or opportunity to express themselves. Some were afraid of making mistakes, some depended heavily on memorisation, and others had never been encouraged to share their own opinions.

We started Budding Voice to create a safe and positive space where children can speak, be heard and gradually understand themselves better. For us, self-discovery means helping learners identify what they enjoy, what they are good at, what goals they want to pursue and what limiting beliefs may be stopping them. We then guide them to take action, stay accountable and develop a genuine sense of ownership over their growth.

### What keeps you motivated?

**Alfred's perspective:**

Seeing a child change from quiet, hesitant or closed off into someone who is willing to speak, participate and share genuine ideas is the strongest motivation. Parents' appreciation also reminds us that the work has an impact beyond the classroom.

**The coaches and wider BV team:**

We are motivated by building a place where coaches do not merely deliver lessons. They learn to facilitate, lead, create and take responsibility. We want our people to develop an entrepreneurial mindset, feel a sense of belonging and see Budding Voice as a platform where they can contribute ideas and grow together.

### What are you most proud of?

**Proposed Budding Voice response:**

- The visible growth of children who become more confident, expressive and willing to take initiative.
- Parents recognising the personal care Budding Voice gives to each learner, rather than treating every child in exactly the same way.
- Creating real platforms such as presentations and showcases where learners can apply what they have learned.
- Coaches who understand the Budding Voice vision, become committed to it and begin taking ownership of programme quality and student growth.
- Building a growing community of learners, families, educators and coaches who support one another.

### What challenges do you face today?

**Proposed Budding Voice response:**

- **Public awareness:** many parents still see communication and leadership as optional activities rather than essential life skills.
- **Brand visibility:** Budding Voice has meaningful work, programmes and student stories, but the public may not yet see or understand them consistently.
- **Positioning:** we need to communicate clearly that Budding Voice is more than a conventional public-speaking or speech-memorisation class.
- **Explaining value:** parents may compare fees before understanding the longer-term value of confidence, character, critical thinking and self-expression.
- **Consistency while growing:** as we expand through coaches, locations and education partners, we must protect programme quality and the personal care behind the brand.

## PART 2 — Understand Their Customers

### Who is your favourite customer? Describe them.

**Proposed Budding Voice response:**

Our favourite parent is not necessarily the parent of the most confident child. It is a parent who genuinely wants to understand the child's growth, communicates openly with the coach, values gradual progress and is willing to support consistent attendance and practice.

Our favourite education partner is a school, preschool or centre owner who sees communication development as part of the child's overall growth, works collaboratively with us and is open to building a programme for long-term impact rather than treating it as a one-off activity.

### Why were they easy to work with?

**Proposed Budding Voice response:**

- We share a clear goal for the learner.
- They trust the learning process while still giving honest feedback.
- They communicate changes, concerns and expectations early.
- They understand that confidence and communication improve through consistency, not overnight.
- They allow the child to learn, make mistakes and gradually take ownership.

### Who isn't your ideal customer?

**Proposed Budding Voice response:**

A poor fit would be someone who expects instant transformation, wants the child only to memorise a perfect script or win a trophy, or judges the programme solely by price without considering the learning process. It is also difficult when attendance is inconsistent, the learner is pressured excessively, or there is no openness to communication between the parent, learner and coach.

### What questions do customers ask repeatedly?

**Proposed Budding Voice response:**

- Is the class conducted in English, and can my child join if their English is not strong?
- My child is shy or refuses to speak. Will this programme suit them?
- Which programme or age group is suitable?
- What happens during a typical class?
- How long does the programme take, and how will I see progress?
- Are there homework, assessments, competitions or showcases?
- What are the fees, schedule and available locations?
- Can my child attend a trial class first?
- Do you support children with different learning needs?
- Do you provide programmes for schools, preschools, teenagers, adults or corporate teams?

### What concerns stop customers from buying?

**Proposed Budding Voice response:**

- Uncertainty about whether the child will participate or enjoy the class.
- Difficulty seeing immediate or measurable results.
- Fees, location, travelling time and schedule commitments.
- Comparisons with tuition centres, English classes or lower-priced public-speaking programmes.
- Concern that a shy child may feel pressured or embarrassed.
- Lack of clarity about programme differences and the learner's next step.
- Insufficient public visibility, testimonials or proof of previous student growth.

## PART 3 — Discover Their Brand

### Imagine Budding Voice is a person. How would you describe them?

**Proposed Budding Voice response:**

Budding Voice would be a warm, observant and encouraging mentor. This person is energetic enough to engage children, professional enough to earn parents' trust, and thoughtful enough to listen before giving direction. They do not dominate the conversation; they ask good questions, make learning simple and help others discover the answer.

**Brand personality:**

- Friendly and approachable, but still structured and responsible.
- Energetic and youthful, without looking childish.
- Reliable, caring and attentive to individual growth.
- Innovative and willing to improve how communication is taught.
- Premium in learning experience and personal care, rather than luxurious or exclusive.
- Modern and forward-looking, while remaining grounded in human connection.

### When customers leave the website, how should they feel?

**Proposed Budding Voice response:**

- "They understand my child and the challenges we are facing."
- "This is more than a public-speaking class."
- "I can trust their coaches and learning approach."
- "My child will be supported, not forced or judged."
- "I know which programme to explore and what to do next."
- "Budding Voice feels warm, credible and committed to real growth."

## PART 4 — Sales Process

### Walk me through what happens after someone contacts you.

**Recommended parent enquiry flow:**

1. Visitor arrives on the website and chooses the child's age, challenge or suitable programme.
2. Visitor clicks "Book a Trial" or "WhatsApp Us" and shares basic information: child's age, location, speaking concern and preferred schedule.
3. The team responds, asks a few needs-based questions and recommends the most suitable programme or trial class.
4. The parent receives the class details, schedule, location, fee and what to expect.
5. The learner attends a trial or introductory session.
6. The coach gives a brief observation or feedback and recommends the appropriate next step.
7. The parent confirms enrolment, receives onboarding information and joins the relevant communication group.
8. During the programme, the team maintains parent communication and shares appropriate progress updates.

**Recommended school or corporate enquiry flow:**

1. Decision-maker selects "Partner With Us" or "Corporate Training Enquiry."
2. The website collects the organisation, target participants, objectives, group size and preferred timing.
3. Budding Voice schedules a discovery call or meeting.
4. The team proposes the relevant programme structure, scope, timeline and quotation.
5. After confirmation, Budding Voice aligns on delivery, materials, participant communication and measurement of outcomes.

> ⚠️ **To confirm:** Agree on one primary enquiry owner, expected response time and lead-tracking process before the website launches.

## PART 5 — Biggest Business Problems

### What currently wastes the most time?

**Proposed Budding Voice response:**

- Repeatedly explaining the same programme basics, age groups, fees, schedules and learning outcomes through WhatsApp.
- Following up with enquiries manually across different chats without one clear system.
- Helping parents understand which programme is suitable because the pathways are not yet presented in one central place.
- Searching for photos, videos, testimonials, proposals and programme information across different files or devices.
- Creating public awareness repeatedly because there is no central website that explains the full Budding Voice story.
- Managing inconsistent information when different team members describe programmes in different ways.
- Finding and nurturing new parent, school and corporate leads.

**How the website will help:**

The website should reduce these problems by centralising accurate information, answering common questions, presenting programme pathways clearly and routing each type of enquiry to the correct next step.

## PART 6 — Competitor Review

Open relevant education, enrichment and communication-training websites during the meeting. Use these criteria to guide the discussion.

### What do you like?

**Proposed Budding Voice response:**

- Simple navigation that helps parents find programmes by age or need.
- Real student photos, short videos and transformation stories.
- Clear learning outcomes and class journeys without excessive text.
- Warm, modern visuals with strong credibility.
- Visible testimonials, partner logos and coach profiles.
- One clear call to action on each page.

### What don't you like?

**Proposed Budding Voice response:**

- Crowded pages, too many colours or overly childish graphics.
- Generic stock photos that do not show the real learning experience.
- Websites that focus only on awards, trophies or perfect performances.
- Long academic explanations filled with jargon.
- Hidden fees, unclear age groups or no obvious next step.
- Aggressive sales language or exaggerated promises.

### What would you improve?

**Proposed Budding Voice response:**

- Make programme differences easier to compare.
- Show the learner journey from first class to showcase and continued growth.
- Explain how confidence, character, communication and self-discovery connect.
- Include frequently asked questions based on real parent concerns.
- Use stronger mobile design because many enquiries will come through WhatsApp and social media.
- Provide separate pathways for parents, school partners and corporate clients.

### What would you never copy?

**Proposed Budding Voice response:**

- Claims that every child will become confident immediately.
- A tuition-centre or examination-centre image that conflicts with BV's facilitation approach.
- A design that treats children as products or uses fear to pressure parents.
- Competitor wording, curriculum or visual identity.
- Unconsented child images, videos, recordings or testimonials.

> ⚠️ **To confirm:** Specific competitor websites should be selected and reviewed together during the meeting.

## PART 7 — Vision

### Imagine your dream website. No limits. Describe it.

**Proposed Budding Voice response:**

The dream Budding Voice website feels clean, warm, modern and full of real human energy. It opens with a strong message and a short visual story of learners speaking, thinking, collaborating and growing. Parents can quickly choose a pathway based on age or concern, while school and corporate partners have clearly separated sections.

The website should balance professionalism with personality. It should use real class moments, coach interactions, testimonials and student journeys rather than looking like a generic education template. Every page should be easy to understand on a phone and lead towards one practical action: book a trial, WhatsApp the team, find the right programme or discuss a partnership.

**Key sections:**

- Programme pathways by age: Kaizen, Orator, APEX and relevant customised programmes.
- A clear explanation of the Budding Voice learning approach and four-month, 16-week journey.
- Student growth stories, showcases, parent voices and partner feedback.
- Meet the coaches and the values behind the team.
- Resources that educate parents about confidence, communication and leadership.
- A scalable structure for future locations, partners and programmes.

### If someone visits for only 30 seconds, what ONE message should they remember?

**Recommended core message:**

> Every child has a voice. Budding Voice helps them discover it, express it confidently and grow with purpose.

**Possible homepage headline:**

> Discover Their Voice. Unleash Their Potential.

**Supporting line:**

> Communication and leadership programmes that build confidence, character, critical thinking and meaningful connection.

> ⚠️ **To confirm:** Choose one final headline after the designer tests it against the homepage visuals and target audiences.

## PART 8 — Success Metrics

**Proposed Budding Voice response:**

- More qualified WhatsApp messages, trial-class enquiries and programme consultations.
- Parents arrive with a clearer understanding of the programme before speaking with the team.
- More school, preschool and corporate partnership enquiries.
- A higher percentage of enquiries progress to trial classes, meetings or enrolment.

## PART 9 — Wrap-up

### Suggested meeting summary

**Proposed Budding Voice response:**

From today's discussion, my understanding is that Budding Voice does not simply need a website that lists public-speaking classes. The website needs to communicate the deeper purpose of the organisation: helping learners discover their voice, understand themselves, build confidence and character, and take ownership of their growth.

The website should position Budding Voice as a warm, trusted and forward-looking Communication & Leadership Centre. It needs to explain the programme pathways clearly, show authentic student growth, answer common parent questions and create separate enquiry journeys for families, education partners and corporate clients.

The priority is to make it easy for visitors to trust the brand and take the next step through WhatsApp, a trial-class enquiry or a partnership discussion. The final proposal should therefore cover the recommended sitemap, homepage message, programme structure, content requirements, enquiry flow, visual direction, timeline and responsibilities.
